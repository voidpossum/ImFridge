## Overview

Game version 0.3.2. Everything below is read from the game's data files (js/data/*.js), so it is always up to date.

**How money works.** There is one number: money earned this run (the score at every review). It comes from: hardware (like Cookie Clicker buildings: every point of processing in Mining earns $0.30 a second, half before the SodaCoin Wallet); cans sold in person and by drone (every soda sells for your price + its own extra); clicks (processing too: the slider splits each click into money and research); tips and influencer bonuses. Side machines (the new park) add a % of all of it while their condition is true.

**Fans and drones.** Every can you sell brings a new fan with chance 0.3 ÷ (1 + fans ÷ 100), so fans come slower the more you have. Every fan orders 0.02 cans per second online (at most 40 orders wait). Orders walk to your line when there is room; drones deliver the rest, each with a can from your machine (no cans: the order waits). Extra or expired orders are lost, and each one makes a fan leave with chance 0.05.

**Moving.** After the Chapter 1 goal you can move to the new park without a reset: you keep everything. That run uses the new park's movedK / movedGrow for rival-only mods (it has no new Refresh Points yet).

**Reviews.** At the end of every month (4 weeks). Last place = a strike; 3 strikes in a row = reset. Not being last: a card (1 of 3) and a cash bonus of 25% of the month's earnings.

**Chapters.** Chapter 1: earn $30,000 in one run (or reset once). Chapter 2 (the new park): earn $3,000,000 in one run there.

**Resets (prestige).** Refresh Points = floor(0.28008 × cube root of the run's money in cents) + reviews survived. Every Refresh Point ever earned: +2% to all processing. Spend them in the Refresh tree.
